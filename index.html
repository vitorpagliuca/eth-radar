Model: on each 15-min candle where open interest rose, the increase × typical price × multiplier becomes new positions; side split 70/30 (up candle), 30/70 (down), 50/50; liquidation = entry×(1∓1/L±m). $5 bands. A band disappears once price trades through it. Positions below 5x liquidate more than 20% away, outside these maps.

Model estimate, not real positions. The liquidation maps are computed from open interest, price and fixed leverage assumptions; exchanges do not publish where real liquidations sit. The grey strip shows only real liquidations published by public feeds (Binance publishes at most one per second, so the total is understated). The options map uses real open interest from Deribit and Bybit. Analysis tool only: it places no orders and is not investment advice.
